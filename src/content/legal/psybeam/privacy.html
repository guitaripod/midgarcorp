Psybeam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Psybeam translates what you and the person you are talking to say, out loud and in real time. It has no sign-up, no advertising inside the app and no tracking. The audio of a conversation goes from your phone straight to OpenAI to be translated and does not pass through Midgar's servers. What Midgar's servers keep is an anonymous account for your minutes balance, an anonymous Midgar ID, the link between that account and the ID, the App Attest key for the app with its public key and receipt, hashes of your sign-in tokens, the per-device key, a record of each translation session's length, a record each time a translation is refused for lack of minutes, the ad campaign details described under Advertising measurement, what is needed to recognise your purchases, and, if you linked Sign in with Apple in an earlier version, Apple's identifier and email address.

In short

  • While you hold a talk button, the microphone audio is streamed from your phone directly to OpenAI, which translates it and sends the translated speech back. You are asked for your consent before the first conversation and can withdraw it in Settings.
  • The text of a conversation appears on your screen and is not saved or uploaded. Psybeam keeps no history of what was said.
  • The app keeps three anonymous identifiers: an account key for your minutes balance, a random per-device key kept in your Keychain, which survives reinstalling the app, and a Midgar ID that also works in Midgar's other apps. None of them holds your name. The minutes, session, purchase and advertising records described below are kept against them, and an email address is kept only for an account that linked Sign in with Apple, as described under Identity.
  • Purchases are made through Apple. Our own server verifies them and keeps the record of what you bought.
  • Your location is read on your phone to suggest the other person's language and to note your country in the diagnostic log on your phone. It is never sent to us.
  • Nothing is used for tracking or to target you with ads, nothing is sold, and no data is given to third parties for their own use.

Who we are

Psybeam is published by Midgar Oy, a company in Finland, which is the controller of the information described here. You can reach us at marcus@midgarcorp.cc.

AI processing and your audio

To provide the app's core feature, the microphone audio of your conversation is streamed in real time, over an encrypted connection, from your phone directly to OpenAI's real-time translation service. OpenAI translates it, also turns it into the short line that shows what was heard, and sends the translated speech back to your phone. The audio is not sent to our servers and we never receive a copy of it or of the translation. The audio is used only to produce the translation and is not used by us to train AI models; OpenAI handles it under its own privacy and security commitments, which you can read on openai.com.

Before a conversation starts, our server asks OpenAI for a short-lived key for that one session and tells it which language to translate into. Your phone uses that key to connect to OpenAI. Our server keeps a record of each session: a random session id, which anonymous account it belongs to, the minutes reserved and the minutes used, and the time. The record holds no audio, no text and no language. It is deleted with your account.

The person you are talking to is translated by OpenAI in the same way. Tell them that the audio is processed in the cloud; the screen shows a Cloud AI notice for that reason.

Identity

Psybeam works without an account. On first launch we create an anonymous, device-based account that holds your minutes balance. Apart from an email address kept for an earlier Sign in with Apple link, it holds no name, email address or phone number. To check that the account belongs to a genuine Apple device, the app gets a DeviceCheck token from Apple and sends it to our server, which checks it with Apple and does not keep it. Psybeam does not offer Sign in with Apple in this version, so the account Psybeam creates holds no Apple identifier or email address. If you linked Sign in with Apple in an earlier version, we still hold the identifier Apple gave us and the email address Apple provided, which may be Apple's private relay address, until you delete your account. This version no longer offers the link, and we never receive your Apple password. Another Midgar app that offers Sign in with Apple can store one for you under the Midgar ID you share with it, and that link is kept with the Midgar ID until the ID is deleted, so deleting your Psybeam account does not remove it. When you delete your account in Settings, this account, your minutes balance, the session records and everything linked to it are deleted from our server.

The anonymous Midgar ID

The app also asks our server for an anonymous Midgar ID: a random identifier, and a random secret that proves the ID is yours. The server keeps the ID, a hash of the secret and the date it was created. The app keeps both in your iCloud Keychain, so the same ID follows you across your own devices and across Midgar's other apps. When the ID is created or joined the app may ask Apple's App Attest service to confirm that the request comes from a genuine copy of the app; we receive Apple's attestation and nothing about you. The app sends a sign-in token made from the ID with its calls to our identity, AI and purchase ledger services, including the call that lists the Midgar apps you already use. Our AI service uses the token to link your minutes account to the ID, and we keep that link until you delete the account. The Midgar ID itself is not removed by Delete Account in Psybeam, because your other Midgar apps share it. To have it deleted, email marcus@midgarcorp.cc.

Purchases

Minute packs are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases normally carry your anonymous Midgar ID as Apple's app account token. A purchase made while your Midgar ID cannot be read goes without it, and the purchase ledger attaches it to your Midgar ID when it reports the purchase. The app sends the signed transaction record Apple issues (which pack, the price, the currency, the storefront or country of purchase, the dates and Apple's transaction identifiers) to Midgar's purchase ledger at api.midgarcorp.cc, which keeps it linked to your Midgar ID so that your purchases can be restored in your other Midgar apps, and Apple's own server notifications about your purchases reach the same ledger. Our server checks the signed transaction of a minute pack before it credits your balance. From version 1.2.0, neither the app nor our servers send anything about your purchases to RevenueCat; earlier versions have RevenueCat validate purchases, and it receives the purchase receipt from Apple, an anonymous identifier that it generates and your IP address, but not your name or email address. We never receive your payment-card details.

On-device data

Your language choices, your settings and your consent are stored on your phone. The text of a conversation is shown on screen and kept in memory only while it is displayed. A diagnostic log is written on the phone too, recording errors, the language each side translates into, a session identifier, the languages your location suggests and, when location access is allowed, the country your phone is in, never what was said or any token. It does not leave your phone, and it is erased when you delete your account.

Location

If you grant location access, the app asks iOS for your location each time a conversation starts while access is allowed, and when you allow it. Depending on whether Precise Location is on in iOS Settings, this is your precise or approximate location. Your phone sends it to Apple's geocoding service, which gives the country you are in. While the Auto-detect switch in Settings is on, the app suggests that country's language. With the switch off, your phone still reads your location and turns it into a country for the diagnostic log, but suggests nothing. The location and the country are not sent to Midgar or to OpenAI and are not stored on our servers. You can withdraw the permission in iOS Settings to stop the reads.

Advertising measurement

We may promote Psybeam with ads on the App Store. Until our server has recorded Apple's answer, or for five launches if none comes, the app sends Apple's short-lived attribution token to our server each time it starts, whether or not you installed the app after tapping one of those ads. Our server asks Apple which campaign, ad group and search keyword produced the install, if any, and the country the App Store served it in, never who you are. We keep only a fingerprint of the token, not the token itself. Apple's App Store attribution includes no advertising identifier and no device identifier, which is why it requires no tracking permission. We keep those campaign details alongside your anonymous account to measure which App Store ads lead to installs and purchases, and never use them to target you or to show you ads. A purchase made after an install that came from an ad is recorded with that campaign and keyword. We receive nothing that could recognise you in any other app or website, and we never share it.

More Apps

Settings has a Midgar universe card that opens a view of Midgar's other apps. It fetches the app list from midgar-catalog.guitaripod.workers.dev and asks Apple's public iTunes lookup service for current icons, prices and ratings; the country code of your device's region setting is sent so prices are correct for your region. It reports to api.midgarcorp.cc when the view is opened, and which of its tiles were displayed, tapped, loaded or launched, or opened as an overlay. That report contains no identifier of any kind and not your Midgar ID: only the event, the promoted app's App Store id, the country code of your device's region setting, where in the app the screen was opened, and the app's name, version and platform. Our server stores a running total per day, event, app, platform, app major.minor version, the detail each count names (such as the promoted app or the placement) and, for active counts, install week, so there is no per-person or per-device record. This is product-interaction data, used only to decide which of our apps to promote, never to track you or to target you with ads.

The app also sends three more kinds of anonymous count to Midgar's servers in the same way: one "active" count the first time you use the app on each day, in each week and in each month, which tells us how many people use the app; one count each time the credit store opens, naming only whether it was opened from a feature or a limit; and one count for each purchase that names only the product bought. Like the More Apps report, they carry no identifier and are stored only as running totals per day, event, app, platform, app major.minor version, the detail each count names and, for active counts, install week. They are used only to understand how the app is used, never for advertising or tracking.

Midgar's server runs on Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs such as your IP address for a limited time. We do not use them for anything else.

Midgar may publish aggregate statistics built from anonymous counts, such as weekly active installs, which never identify a person.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It contains no advertising and no user-to-user communication.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show advertising inside the app, use advertising identifiers, run third-party analytics or crash-reporting software, or ask for tracking permission. We do not use your data to train AI models.

How long we keep data

  • The audio and the translation: not kept by us. OpenAI handles them under its own commitments.
  • Your anonymous account, its minutes balance, its device key, the session records, the paywall and ad campaign records, any Apple identifier and email address from an earlier Sign in with Apple link, and its link to your Midgar ID: until you delete your account in Settings.
  • The Midgar ID record, with its App Attest key, public key, receipt and sign-in token hashes: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Your settings: only on your phone, until you delete the app. Your log: only on your phone, until you delete your account or the app.
  • Purchase records: Midgar's purchase ledger keeps them as financial records, linked to your Midgar ID until that ID is deleted, after which nothing links them to a live ID. RevenueCat keeps the records it received from earlier versions of the app under its own policy. Apple's records of your purchases: kept by Apple.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw your consent to cloud AI translation at any time in Settings; translation is unavailable until you grant it again. You can delete your account in Settings at any time, which removes your anonymous account, your minutes and the records linked to it from our server, erases the diagnostic log on your phone and withdraws your consent to cloud translation. Your settings stay on your phone until you delete the app. Deleting the app alone does not remove the server-side account or your Midgar ID, which stays in your iCloud Keychain. Where privacy law such as the GDPR gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or move information, or to object to its use, contact us at marcus@midgarcorp.cc and we will help. We process this information to provide the translation and purchases you ask for. Midgar's servers run on Cloudflare as our hosting provider, which may process a request in any of its data centres, and OpenAI is our AI provider. You may complain to your local data protection authority; in Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
