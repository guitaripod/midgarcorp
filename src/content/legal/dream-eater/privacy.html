Dream Eater Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Dream Eater is a private dream journal. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, who handles it and how to delete it.

What stays on your device

Your dreams, voice recordings, transcripts, journal, tags, birth details and the sky data worked out from them are stored on your device and are not uploaded to us.

Readings

Only when you ask for a reading, the dream text, the options you chose for it and short summaries of earlier dreams you had read are sent to Dream Eater's server, which passes them to Google's Gemini model to write the reading. Our server keeps no dream text, prompt or reading afterwards: it passes the request on, returns the answer to your device and forgets it. You can withdraw your permission for readings at any time in Settings, then Privacy, and everything else keeps working.

Art

Art is made from a short scene summary, not from your dream text. The summary goes through our server to Google's image model, and the image is returned to your device and is not stored on our server.

What our server keeps

  • An anonymous identifier and key for your device. There is no name and no email address.
  • Your credit balance and the ledger of credits granted, used and bought.
  • Counters for your weekly free readings.
  • The Apple Ads attribution verdict, if you installed the app after tapping one of our ads (see Advertising measurement below).

Purchases

Credit packs and the optional subscription are sold through Apple In-App Purchase and processed with RevenueCat. We receive purchase records (which product and a transaction identifier) to credit your balance or unlock the subscription. We never receive your payment-card details.

Who handles data

  • Google (Gemini): writes readings and paints art from the text described above.
  • RevenueCat: processes purchases and tells our server whether you have the subscription.
  • Apple: StoreKit for purchases, and Apple Ads attribution that tells us which ad campaign an install came from.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our server.

Google handles the text sent for a reading or for art under its own terms and privacy policy. Under its terms for the paid Gemini API, Google does not use those prompts and responses to improve its products, and it keeps a log of them for a limited time solely to detect abuse. We do not use your dreams to train AI models, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Advertising measurement

We may promote Dream Eater with ads on the App Store. If you installed the app after tapping one of those ads, Apple tells our server which campaign, ad group, and search keyword produced the install, and the country the App Store served it in, never who you are. Apple's App Store attribution includes no advertising identifier and no device identifier, which is why it requires no tracking permission. We keep those campaign details alongside your anonymous identity purely to see which ads pay for themselves, we receive nothing that could recognise you in any other app or website, and we never share it.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy, then Delete all data. This removes the dreams and everything else stored on your device, and deletes your server identity, credit ledger and weekly counters. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a subscription: manage or cancel that in iOS Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies.

Children

Dream Eater is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show advertising inside the app, or use third-party tracking or advertising identifiers.
