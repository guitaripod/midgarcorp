App of the Dead Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

App of the Dead is a quiz app about the world's religions and what they teach about the afterlife. It has no accounts to create, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics about what you study. Your lessons, quiz answers, Judgment, XP, streaks and achievements stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and the Oracle runs on your phone without saving your conversations. What reaches Midgar's servers is an anonymous Midgar ID, the anonymous More Apps and usage counts, and what is needed to recognise your purchases. Purchases themselves are handled by Apple and recorded by Midgar's purchase ledger.

In short

  • Your progress, answers, Judgment, streaks and achievements are stored on your device. Oracle conversations are not saved at all. We never receive any of it.
  • The app keeps an anonymous Midgar ID, a random identifier with no name, email address or other detail about you, in your iCloud Keychain.
  • Purchases are made through Apple, and Midgar's server keeps Apple's records of this app's purchases. When you delete your Midgar ID, those records are no longer tied to any account, though they still carry the deleted ID.
  • The app sends anonymous counts with no identifier: opening the More Apps screen in Settings and its app tiles (shown, tapped, opened from the screen, loaded), how many people use the app each day, week and month, how often the purchase screen is shown and from where in the app, and each purchase by the product bought. You can turn these counts off in Settings, About, Usage Counts.
  • Nothing is used for advertising or tracking, nothing is sold, and no data is given to third parties for their own use.

Who we are

App of the Dead is published by Midgar Oy, a company in Finland. For the information described under The anonymous Midgar ID, Purchases and More Apps below, Midgar Oy is the controller. You can reach us at marcus@midgarcorp.cc.

What stays on your device

Lessons completed, quiz answers, mistakes to review, XP, streaks, achievements, reading progress, your answers to the Judgment, how many times you consulted each deity and the Oracle's explanations of book passages and of lesson key terms that you asked for are stored inside the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Oracle conversations are kept only while a conversation is open and are never written to storage. The saved explanations are single answers, not conversations. If you start a Premium Week, the date it started is kept in this device's keychain, so that reinstalling the app does not start another one. A diagnostic log is written there too, recording errors, the app version and device model, never your questions to the Oracle, your answers or any token. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it. Deleting the app removes what it kept in its own storage; items in the keychain, described here and under The anonymous Midgar ID, can stay until you remove them.

The Oracle

Oracle answers are generated by a language model that runs entirely on your device. What you ask the Oracle is never sent to us or to any AI service. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the Oracle uses Apple's on-device model, under Apple's terms, and nothing leaves the device. On other iPhones the model file is downloaded once from Hugging Face, which sees an ordinary file request with your IP address and nothing about your questions.

iCloud and Game Center

If iCloud is on, your progress and your Judgment sync between your own devices through your iCloud account. That is Apple's service, under Apple's terms, and we never receive the data. If you sign in to Game Center, achievements and leaderboard entries are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional, and declining it does not limit the learning content.

The anonymous Midgar ID

The first time the app starts, it asks our server for an anonymous Midgar ID: a random identifier, and a random secret that proves the ID is yours. The server keeps the ID, the app that created it, the attestation level it reached (App Attest, development or unverified), a hash of the secret, and the dates the secret was created and last changed. For each device that passes App Attest it keeps the key's identifier and public key, the bundle id, whether the key belongs to a production or development build, Apple's attestation receipt where Apple returns one, the key's use counter and the date it was last used. It keeps a hash of each sign-in token, with the app it was issued to, the device key it was issued for when there is one, and when it was created, used and expires. If you link the ID to an Apple Account with Sign in with Apple, in any Midgar app that offers it, the server keeps Apple's user identifier for that account and the date it was linked. When two Midgar IDs are merged, the server keeps a record of the merge. The server also keeps a credit wallet for the ID, which holds any AI credits bought in Midgar apps and is kept by Midgar's intelligence service. The app keeps the ID and the secret in your iCloud Keychain, so the same ID follows you across your own devices and across Midgar's other apps. There is no name, email address, phone number, location or device name in it. When the app creates or joins the ID it may ask Apple's App Attest service to confirm that the request comes from a genuine copy of the app; we receive Apple's attestation and nothing about you. The ID is used to recognise your purchases and, on the More Apps screen, to show which Midgar apps you already use.

Purchases

Learning paths, deity packs, Oracle Wisdom, Ultimate Enlightenment and the Premium subscription are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment and billing, and we never receive your card details or your Apple Account details. Each purchase carries your Midgar ID as Apple's app account token. Midgar's server receives Apple's signed records of this app's purchases: which product, the price and currency, the dates, the transaction identifiers, your App Store country, whether a discount offer was used, whether it was bought by you or shared through Family Sharing, whether it was refunded or revoked and when, and for subscriptions Apple's renewal details: whether it renews, the product it renews to, the renewal date, any billing retry or grace period, and why it is set to end. From version 1.4.12, neither the app nor our servers send anything about your purchases to RevenueCat; earlier versions have RevenueCat validate purchases, and it receives the purchase receipt from Apple, an anonymous identifier that RevenueCat generates and your IP address, but not your name or email address. Where the server can match a purchase to your Midgar ID it keeps the two linked, so that what you bought can be recognised in other Midgar apps. The app asks the server, with the Midgar ID as a sign-in token, which purchases belong to it. We keep these records for as long as Apple's financial records require.

Midgar's server runs on Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs such as your IP address for a limited time. We do not use them for anything else.

More Apps

Settings has a More Apps card that opens a view of Midgar's other apps. It fetches the app list from midgar-catalog.guitaripod.workers.dev and asks Apple's public iTunes lookup service for current icons, prices and ratings; the country of your device's Region setting is sent so prices are correct for your region. It reports to api.midgarcorp.cc which app tiles were displayed, which were tapped, which installed Midgar app was opened from the screen and when another app's App Store page finished loading. That report contains no identifier of you or your device, and never your Midgar ID: only the event, the promoted app's App Store id, the country of your device's Region setting, where in the app the screen was opened, and the app's name, version and platform. Our server stores a running total per day, event, app, platform and app version (major and minor number), so there is no per-person or per-device record. This is product-interaction data, used only to decide which of our apps to promote, never for advertising or tracking. When you open an app from More Apps that is not installed on this device but runs on it, its App Store page opens inside this app. Apple then receives a campaign token that names this app and Settings, together with Midgar's App Store provider token, and uses them to credit a download to its source in Midgar's App Store analytics. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that page. On the More Apps screen, the Midgar ID is sent as a sign-in token only with the separate request that lists which Midgar apps you already use and what you own in them, so the screen does not suggest an app you already have.

Midgar may publish aggregate statistics built from anonymous counts, such as weekly active installs, which never identify a person.

The app also sends two more kinds of anonymous count to Midgar's servers in the same way: one "active" count the first time you use the app on each day, in each week and in each month, which tells us how many people use the app, and one count for each purchase that names only the product bought. Like the More Apps report, they carry no identifier and are stored only as running totals per day, event, app, platform and app version (major and minor number), with the placement, product, period or promoted app each count names. The active count is also totalled by the week the app was installed on your device. They are used only to understand how the app is used, never for advertising or tracking.

You can turn all of these counts off in Settings, About, Usage Counts. While the switch is off, the app sends none of them, including the More Apps counts, and it discards any it has not yet sent. Opening that screen also asks api.midgarcorp.cc for Midgar's public numbers for the latest finished week. That request carries no identifier, and the switch does not affect it. The request that reads the apps and purchases tied to your Midgar ID for More Apps is separate, and the switch does not control it either.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It contains no advertising, no user-to-user communication and no links to unmoderated content.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, use advertising identifiers, run third-party analytics or crash-reporting software, or ask for tracking permission. We do not use your data to train AI models.

How long we keep data

  • Your progress, answers and Judgment: only on your device and in your iCloud, until you delete the app or reset your progress. Oracle conversations: only while the conversation is open. Saved Oracle explanations: only on your device, until you delete the app. The app keeps at most 500 and drops the least recently used first.
  • Your Midgar ID and its keys and sign-in records: until you delete it, as described below. An ID that was never verified with App Attest, has no valid sign-in and is not linked to Sign in with Apple is deleted automatically once it is more than 30 days old. That automatic deletion does not erase a credit wallet the intelligence service holds for the ID.
  • Purchase records: for as long as Apple's financial records require, even after your Midgar ID is deleted. After that they still show the deleted Midgar ID they were bought with, but are linked to no account.
  • RevenueCat's records of purchases made with earlier versions of the app: kept by RevenueCat under its own policy.
  • Apple's records of your purchases, iCloud data and Game Center data: kept by Apple under its own policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes what it kept in its own storage on your device. To delete your Midgar ID from our server, open Settings in the app and choose Delete Midgar ID, from version 2.0.1. That deletes the ID, its keys and its sign-in records, erases any credit wallet the intelligence service holds for it, and removes the account's claim to your purchases; the purchase records themselves stay, still showing the deleted ID, as described under How long we keep data. You can also email marcus@midgarcorp.cc, tell us the app and the approximate date of your first purchase, and we will delete your Midgar ID for you. We delete every server record that links you to an account. Purchase records that we keep for Apple's financial records stay, still showing the deleted Midgar ID they were bought with, but are linked to no account. We cannot change Apple's own records or cancel or refund a purchase: do that through Apple. Data in your iCloud is yours and is managed in Settings.

Your rights

Because we keep very little information about you, most requests are answered by the controls above. Where privacy law such as the GDPR gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or move information, or to object to its use, contact us and we will help. We process the Midgar ID and purchase records to provide and account for what you buy. Processing takes place in the European Union and the United States, by Cloudflare as our hosting provider. You may complain to your local data protection authority; in Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
