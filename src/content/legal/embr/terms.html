Embr Terms of Use

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Embr is an independent client for watching Twitch streams and chat, with Embr Studio for streamers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Twitch Interactive, Inc. By using Embr you agree to these terms and to Twitch's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

User-generated content

Embr displays live chat and other content created by Twitch users. Embr does not create, endorse, or control that content. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Embr filters objectionable content automatically, and lets you mute keywords, report any message, and block any user. Blocked and reported users are hidden from your chat instantly and flagged to the developer. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours: offending content is removed and offending users are ejected. Twitch's own moderation and reporting tools also apply.

Acceptable use

Do not use Embr to harass, threaten, or abuse others, to post unlawful content, or to circumvent Twitch's terms. Streams play through Twitch's official embedded player, including any advertising Twitch serves.

Embr Studio

Embr Studio shows your own Twitch channel, or one you moderate, through Twitch's official API with the permissions you grant when you sign in. What you do in Studio, such as running or snoozing an ad, posting a message, pinning, changing chat modes, turning on Shield Mode, timing out or banning someone, is done on Twitch as you, under Twitch's terms. You can withdraw Embr's access at any time by logging out, deleting your account in Settings or disconnecting Embr in your Twitch settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Founding Member and Studio Pro are optional and sold through Apple In-App Purchase under Apple's terms. Watching, chat and moderating your own channel never need them.

Studio Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings; cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for. Every streamer gets one free week of Studio Pro inside the app from the first live stream Studio sees; it needs no purchase, ends on its own and never turns into a paid subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple. One purchase covers Embr on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV signed in to the same Apple Account.
