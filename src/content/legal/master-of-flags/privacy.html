Master of Flags Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Master of Flags is a flag and capitals quiz for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch. It is open to children. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no account to create. Your quiz progress stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The only things that reach our server are an anonymous Midgar ID, the technical details that every request carries, and, when you buy Pro or restore a purchase, or left a tip in an older version of the app, the purchase record and Apple's notices about it. We use them to restore and verify what you bought, to handle refunds, to hand each tip and tip refund to Midgar's AI service, to send a sale alert that carries no Midgar ID, and to limit how many requests one network can send.

In short

  • Your answers, scores, streaks, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device. We never receive them.
  • The app keeps an anonymous Midgar ID, a random identifier with no name, email address or other detail about you, in your iCloud Keychain.
  • When you buy Pro or restore a purchase, or leave a tip in an older version of the app, the purchase record from Apple is sent to Midgar's own server, linked to that Midgar ID, so that your purchases can be verified and restored. Midgar's AI service also receives each tip and tip refund, and a sale alert without your Midgar ID goes to Telegram. Apple receives the Midgar ID with each purchase when the app has one.
  • Nothing is used for analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing is sold. Some data reaches Apple, Cloudflare and Telegram, as described below.

Who we are

Master of Flags is published by Midgar Oy, a company in Finland. For the information described under The anonymous Midgar ID, Purchases and Requests to our server below, Midgar Oy is the controller. You can reach us at marcus@midgarcorp.cc.

What the app stores on your device

Everything about how you play is kept in the app's private storage on your device, and in a storage area shared only with the app's own widgets and Apple Watch app. It is not uploaded to us. It includes your answer history, your Daily Challenge results and streak, your Smart Review schedule, your bookmarks, your points and levels, and your settings, such as reminder time, appearance and app icon. In Settings, Privacy, you can see what is stored and erase your progress on that device and its copy in your iCloud.

iCloud and Game Center

If iCloud is on, your streak and freezes, Daily Challenge results, bookmarks, flag game scores and player levels, and settings sync between your own devices through your iCloud account. That is Apple's service, under Apple's terms, and we never receive the data. If you are signed in to Game Center, the app reports scores and achievements to Apple, which shows them under your Game Center settings. Opening a Game Center screen asks a grown-up first.

The anonymous Midgar ID

The first time the app starts, it asks our server for an anonymous Midgar ID: a random identifier, and a random secret that proves the ID is yours. The server keeps the ID, a hash of the secret, the date it was created, the app that created it, its status, the Apple sign-in link if you make one, the public keys and attestation receipts of its App Attest keys, and, for each session, a hash of its refresh token, the app it was issued to and its expiry. The app keeps the ID and the secret in your iCloud Keychain, so that the same ID follows you across your own devices and across Midgar's other apps. There is no name, email address, phone number, location or device name in it, and it cannot be traced to you without your devices. The ID is used to tie your purchases to you and to keep your sessions working, and our server limits how often a new device can join it. Apple receives it with each purchase when the app has one (see Purchases). On Apple Watch the app holds no Midgar ID and sends nothing to our server.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase through Apple In-App Purchase. Older versions of the app also had a Tip Jar that sold optional tips the same way; the current version has none and sells only Pro. Apple handles payment and billing, and we never receive your card details or your Apple Account details. Each purchase is made with your Midgar ID attached whenever the app has one, so Apple's own record of that purchase carries the ID. Apple keeps that record under its own policies.

When a purchase is verified or restored, the app sends Midgar's server the signed transaction record from Apple: which product, the price, the dates, the order identifiers, your App Store country and the Midgar ID. Our server uses it to confirm whether Pro is unlocked and to restore it on your other devices and after a reinstall. Restoring a purchase also works without our server, through Apple.

Apple also sends our server its App Store Server Notifications about purchases, refunds, revocations and reversals. We store them alongside the purchase record, which holds the Midgar ID. When Apple reports a tip purchase or a tip refund from a version that still had the Tip Jar, the record is also passed to Midgar's AI service, which credits its own credit packs. Master of Flags has no credit pack, so the service refuses it and credits nothing.

For each sale, Midgar's ledger queues the app, the product, the price, the currency and the buyer's App Store country. A script run by Midgar's founder reads that queue and posts each sale to Telegram, so that a sale can be seen as it happens. The message shows the app, the product, the price and the buyer's App Store country. It does not include your Midgar ID. Telegram handles the message under its own terms.

Requests to our server

Each request to our server carries the app's name and version, the app's ID and a random request ID. A request made with your Midgar ID session also carries a short-lived access token that names the ID. Cloudflare works out the country your connection comes from, and that country is passed to our server with each request. Our server uses your IP address to limit how many requests one network can send. Midgar's server runs on Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs, including your IP address, for a limited time. We do not use those logs for anything else.

Children

Master of Flags is in the Apple Kids Category and is designed for children. We do not ask for or collect a child's name, email address or precise location. Each request to our server carries the connection's IP address, which we use only to limit request rates, and a country that Cloudflare derives from it, which no Midgar service keeps for this app. The anonymous Midgar ID and the purchase record are used to provide and check what was bought and to keep your sessions working. Two other uses are described under Purchases: the sale alert, which carries no Midgar ID, and the hand-off of tip purchases and tip refunds to Midgar's AI service, which refuses them. Every purchase, every link out of the app, every share and every Game Center screen asks a grown-up first through a parental gate, except the button that opens your device's Settings when notifications are off. There are no ads and no chat. The only social features are Game Center's leaderboards and challenges, which Apple provides. They can show other players' names and scores, and a challenge lets a player send one to another player. Each one opens only after a grown-up passes the parental gate.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, use advertising identifiers or third-party tracking, or run analytics software in the app. The app does not ask for tracking permission. We do not use your data to train AI models. Apart from Apple, which receives the Midgar ID with each purchase when the app has one, Telegram, which receives the sale details described under Purchases without the Midgar ID, and Cloudflare, which hosts our server, we do not share the Midgar ID or purchase records with any third party.

Midgar may publish aggregate statistics built from anonymous counts, such as weekly active installs, which never identify a person; Master of Flags sends no usage counts, so it adds nothing to them.

How long we keep data

  • Your progress and settings: only on your device and in your iCloud, until you erase them in Settings, Privacy, or delete the app.
  • The Midgar ID record: until you ask us to delete it, or, if the ID was created more than 30 days ago without App Attest, has no Apple sign-in link and has had no session issued or renewed for 30 days, until our server deletes it automatically. That automatic deletion removes the ID record and the claims that tie purchases to it, but leaves any credit wallet in place.
  • Your purchase records: we keep them, with no set deletion date. Deleting your Midgar ID does not remove them, as described below.
  • Apple's records of your purchases, iCloud data and Game Center data: kept by Apple under its own policies.

Deleting your data

In Settings, Privacy, "Delete all progress" erases your quiz progress on that device and its copy in your iCloud. It does not erase the progress on your other devices, which may sync back to this one. The app does not yet have a button that deletes the Midgar ID. Our server deletes a Midgar ID only on a request from a device that holds its credential or App Attest key, so we cannot delete one from an email alone. If you email marcus@midgarcorp.cc from any address and tell us the app and the approximate date of your first purchase, and we will tell you what we can do. Deleting the Midgar ID removes its record from our identity service, the claims that tie purchases made without the ID to it, and any credit wallet kept for it. Your purchase records stay on our server. They still show the Midgar ID they were made with, but nothing links that ID to a live identity. We keep them because they are records of Apple's transactions. We cannot change Apple's own records, and we cannot cancel or refund a purchase: do that through Apple. Data in your iCloud is yours and is managed in iOS or macOS Settings.

Your rights

Because we keep very little information about you, most requests are answered by the controls above. Where privacy law, such as the GDPR or COPPA, gives you or your child's parent rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or move information, or to object to its use, contact us and we will help. We process the Midgar ID and purchase records to provide and support what you buy. You may complain to your local data protection authority; in Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
